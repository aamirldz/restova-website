# Restova SEO Experiments Log

**Purpose:** Track isolated A/B style SEO tests. Change ONE variable, measure, and decide.

## Format
- **HYPOTHESIS:** What we believe will happen
- **VARIABLE:** The single thing we are changing (e.g., Title Tag)
- **CONTROL:** The original state
- **CHANGE:** The new state
- **START DATE:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **MEASUREMENT WINDOW:** e.g., 14 days, 30 days
- **RESULT:** The data outcome
- **DECISION:** Keep, Revert, or Iterate
