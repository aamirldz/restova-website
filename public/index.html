FAQ

Common questions.

Restova is a complete restaurant operating system that includes a POS (Point of Sale) for billing, a Captain App for tableside ordering, and a Kitchen Display System (KDS) for kitchen order management. All three products work together in real time.

POS sits at the billing counter — handles tables, billing, payments, and reports. Captain App is for waiters — they take orders at the table on their phone. KDS is the kitchen screen — chefs see orders in real time and mark them as ready. All three sync automatically.

Yes. Restova is built offline-first. All data is stored locally on each device. When internet drops, you can continue billing, printing KOTs, and managing tables. When connection returns, all changes sync automatically to the cloud.

Restova uses WebSocket connections between all devices. When a Captain adds an item to a table, the POS and KDS update within milliseconds. No page refresh needed. Changes propagate to all connected devices automatically.

Restova works with standard thermal printers that support browser printing. You can configure separate printers for KOT (kitchen) and customer bills. No special hardware purchase required.

Register your restaurant on our website. Our team will contact you within 24 hours. We set up your Restova instance with your menu, table layout, tax configuration, and staff accounts. Your restaurant-specific POS is deployed on Cloudflare's global network for maximum speed and reliability.

Restova is designed for dine-in restaurants, cafes, QSRs, cloud kitchens, and multi-outlet chains across India. Whether you have 5 tables or 50, the system scales to your needs.

All data is stored on Cloudflare's global network with D1 database technology. Each restaurant gets its own isolated database. Device authentication uses JWT tokens, and admin access requires SHA-256 hashed credentials. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
